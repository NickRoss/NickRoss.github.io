A workshop on data strategy for startups in the [Polsky Center's Transform Data Science and AI Accelerator](https://polsky.uchicago.edu/programs-events/transform/) at the University of Chicago. The session covers business strategy fundamentals with a focus on data and AI companies, including the history of business strategy frameworks, how VCs and investors evaluate companies, and practical takeaways for founders.

This is a condensed version of the [full Data Strategy course](/biz_strat/) I developed at USF.
