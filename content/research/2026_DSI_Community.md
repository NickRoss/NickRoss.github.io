The University of Chicago's Data Science Institute celebrated five years of partnership with The 11th Hour Project, highlighting community-centered data science initiatives. Over this period, faculty and students collaborated on more than 250 projects with nonprofits and civic organizations globally and locally, including developing open-source tools for human rights organizations, environmental partnerships on wildfire mitigation, and the Data Science Clinic which has completed over 70 projects.
