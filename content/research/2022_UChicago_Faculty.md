The Data Science Institute announced five new full-time faculty joining for the 2022-2023 academic year, expanding DSI's capabilities in visualization, statistics, machine learning, and data economics. These appointments represent part of a broader University of Chicago initiative that has added 33 data science faculty members since 2017.
