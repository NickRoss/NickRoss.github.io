The University of Chicago's Data Science Institute celebrated the 2024-2025 academic year achievements of its Data Science Clinic program. During the year, 202 students completed 28 projects for clients ranging from industry partners to nonprofits. Notable initiatives included machine learning applications at Fermilab, landslide prediction modeling for Rwanda, and a regulatory compliance chatbot for renewable energy firm Invenergy.
